BharatOpt-X | Sovereign Optimization Architecture
🇮🇳 100% Clean-Room Sovereign Architecture

BharatOpt-X is built completely from scratch in C++23 as a sovereign alternative to IBM CPLEX and FICO Xpress for Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited (MRPL) and the Smart India Hackathon (SIH).

Key Subsystem Engines:

  • Two-Phase Revised Simplex: Native sparse matrix representation (CSR/CSC) with LU-factorization.
  • GNN-assisted branching (MILP): Strong branching heuristic coupled with a Graph Neural Network (GNN) variable selector research prototype.
  • Primal-Dual Interior Point (IPM): Cholesky factorization for convex Quadratic Programming (QP).
  • ADMM Decentralized Consensus: Multi-unit refinery scheduling (CDU, FCC, Hydrocracker) decoupling.
  • GPU Acceleration: Sparse Linear Algebra dispatched via cuSPARSE and cuBLAS.

Included Test Datasets:

01. LP Crude Blending
CDU refinery crude selection with distillation yield bounds.
02. MILP Electronics
Discrete hardware manufacturing assembly scheduling.
03. QP Portfolio Risk
Markowitz mean-variance clean energy infrastructure investments.
04. ADMM Refinery Network
Decentralized hydrogen and steam balance across MRPL units.
